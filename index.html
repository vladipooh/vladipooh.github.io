IT Systems Analyst — City of Kenmore | Public-Sector IT Leadership | Data & AI Background

I am the internal IT lead for the City of Kenmore, Washington — the city's sole in-house IT staff member, responsible for the full technology function: Microsoft 365 and enterprise systems, technology projects, IT and cybersecurity policy, asset management, and staff enablement. I direct the work of our external IT service provider and chair the city's IT Advisory Committee, making sure technology decisions serve residents, staff, and council priorities.

What sets my approach apart is depth in data. Before moving into municipal IT, my background spanned data engineering, business intelligence, and AI research — most recently as a Senior Data & BI Consultant for California's Department of Health Care Services (Medi-Cal), working with pipelines and reporting over more than a billion Medicaid records, and before that in Generative AI trust & safety at Meta. I hold a Ph.D. in Information Technology (Cum Laude) focused on reinforcement learning for Type 1 diabetes control. I bring that same standard to city IT: clean systems, trustworthy data, documented standards, and measurable results.
